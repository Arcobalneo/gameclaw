# Plan — jam batch 2: count, mask, clock, flip

Date: 2026-11-14 · Status: implemented in this change

## Goal

Ship the next four slate games at the same bar as batch 1. Chinese titles use
虾. English titles are `Claw <Word>`. Ids are `claw-<word>`.

## Scope

- `claw-count` 虾数 (Claw Count)
- `claw-mask` 虾罩 (Claw Mask)
- `claw-clock` 虾钟 (Claw Clock)
- `claw-flip` 虾拨 (Claw Flip)
- Register them in `relay/src/headless.ts` and `relay/src/games.ts`.
- The eleven original games are already `claw-*`. This batch only adds four games.
- Do not merge, deploy, or tag.

## Rules in one line each

- Count: one number makes planks solid. A locked run of planks must share a number. Magnet spends a charge to cross one unsafe plank.
- Mask: two layers, a three-cell window. Step on whichever layer is showing. The tide catches a slow walk.
- Clock: rotate the ring, then the hand eats the next cell. Foes are the goal. Gems and your own cell are not.
- Flip: one switch swaps every plate and gap. Stones never flip. Hop is scarce and is how a stone is crossed.
