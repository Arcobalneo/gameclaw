# Plan — 19-game jam slate, first four playable

Date: 2026-11-14 · Status: slate locked; first four games implemented in this change

## Goal

Lock 19 original 虾× concepts that do not overlap the 11 shipped games, and ship
the first four at the current library's bar: deterministic engine, Web MCP and
browser play, ladder score, zh/en, 384×216 cover, tests, Chinese README.

This file is a working slate for the jam. It is not a design-rules document.
Rules stay in `docs/references/ai-native-game-design-rules.md`.

## Scope

- New games use `GAME_VERSION` `4.0.0`. Do not bump the other games.
- New identities are `虾×`, `Claw <Word>`, and `claw-<word>`. The original
  eleven games use the same identity. Their former `shrimp-*` ids are aliases.
- No CLI. Do not add ids to `SKILL.md`.
- Register the four ids in `relay/src/games.ts` and in
  `relay/src/headless.ts` `DEFINITIONS`. Do not otherwise edit relay session
  or MCP transport code.
- The other 15 concepts are specified here and not implemented in this change.

## Inspiration

Read as mechanisms only. No titles, text, characters, or layouts are reused.

- GMTK 2026: a counter that rewrites the level (Numeral), a mask that hides a
  layer (Clipping Mask), cells inside a ticking clock (Around the Clock), one
  button that flips every trap (Toggle Bot).
- GMTK 2025: place moves on a loop and let the loop fight (Rhythmetric /
  Voodloop), keep one object across a short time loop (Night O'Clock), a snake
  that also pushes (Goop Snake), a small arithmetic machine (Cooking by the
  Numbers).
- Older small-game ideas used only as verbs: merge-in-a-well, a clerk with a
  quota, mending against a spread, berth assignment, lantern placement, cargo
  balance, warehouse recall, mirror aiming, braced digging, posted prices, a
  current that pushes pieces.

## Assumptions

- Present state is public. Uncertainty is the next draw, and `detail` states
  the consequence of the action in front of the agent.
- One `act` is one decision. Scenes animate; they do not run a second clock.
- A strong clear should land near the existing ladders (roughly a few hundred
  to the low thousands), and one mistake should cost more than two clean
  successes.
- Humans use the portal's mouse and WASD action selection, plus scene hit
  regions. Agents use the same actions.

## The four to build

### 虾印

Twelve travelers. Kinds are public: 合法, 擦边, 禁行. Edge fares are higher.
Stamp a legal guest or pardon / listen an edge to keep a streak. Stamp an edge
and it counts, but it is a strike and the streak breaks. Refuse a legal guest
and that is a strike too. One pardon waives the fare. Listen costs two patience
and turns an edge legal only when the detail says so. Quota 7, patience 14,
three strikes ends the night. Easy / hard change quota, patience, and the
strike cap only.

Score: fares×12 + clean catches×35 + best streak×22 − strikes×80 − misreads×40.
Sailing with the quota adds 420 + patience×8. A failed night keeps one third.
Abort is 0. A strike costs more than two catches.

### 虾熔

Six columns, six rows. Drop the shown shell. Equal neighbors of 2+ merge into
size+1, then fall, then chain. Every few drops the top shell of each column
slides one step down-tide when the neighbor is no taller. A tall edge spills.
Reach the goal size, or last the drop count without plugging the mouth.

Score: merge points (new size²×6) + best chain×28 + max size×30 − spills×22.
Clearing the well adds 380. Overflow keeps one third of the merge points.

### 虾网

A 5×4 net. Torn cells with two torn neighbors wet a sound knot; a wet knot
touching a tear becomes a tear. Tie spends thread. Cut makes a gap that does
not spread and holds no fish. Haul scores sound knots minus twice the tears,
then two new tears open. Four nights. Eight tears rip the net.

Score: fish×12 + thread left×6 − cuts×18. Filling the basket adds 340. A thin
haul or a rip keeps fish×4. A cut costs more than one fish.

### 虾节

A four-beat phrase. Each round you may change two beats, then commit it
against the next four visible intents. At most two jabs, jabs cannot touch,
and one beat must rest. Slip answers a chop, guard answers a chop, jab answers
a pause, and a feint punishes a slip. Five rounds, or until breath or the
opponent hits zero.

Score: hits×12 + ripostes×30 + blocks×10 + breath×8 − damage taken×28.
A knockout adds 420. Damage taken costs more than two hits. A loss keeps one
third of the hit and riposte points.

## Risks

- A suggest policy that always wins means the puzzle is too loose. Tests require
  a careless line to lose, and they record the suggest line's result per seed.
- Hidden flags in state would make a deduction game a lookup. These four keep
  the current board public and spend randomness only on the next draw.
- English canvas copy must wrap or use the bitmap font. `say()` returns empty
  while a cover is captured.
- i18n smoke fails if any engine string has no catalog pattern. Templates stay
  few and numbered.
- Relay allowlist drift: without the four ids, remote submit returns
  "Unknown game."
