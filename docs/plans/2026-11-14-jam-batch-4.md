## Rules

- Delve: the shaft is public. Dig down or sideways. Leaving an unbraced layer
  collapses it. Braces are fewer than the layers you must leave. One collapse
  costs more than two loads of ore.
- Fair: the customer and yesterday's price are public. Hold, raise by one, or
  cut to their purse. A raise they refuse does not change yesterday. Two
  walkaways close the stall.
- Reef: step or drop an anchor, then the public current pushes anything not
  anchored. A goal counts only where you stand after the push. A sweep costs
  more than two saved anchors.

## Assumptions

- The board stays public. RNG only draws the next shaft, customer, or goal set.
- Suggest is the first action. A careless line loses more often.
